About

Quiet, long-term, minimal

Infini Capital is a quantitative investment firm focused on repeatable, risk-adjusted edges in liquid markets. The process blends systematic analysis and execution across Indian and U.S. markets, spanning equities, ETFs, bullion, futures, and selective event-driven opportunities.

The emphasis remains on process quality, robust risk management, and continuous improvement of the underlying investment framework.
