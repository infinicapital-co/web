01

One shared space

Infini Capital Blackboard is a shared coordination layer for our research and trading systems. Specialized agents can publish tasks, observations, results, messages, and status updates in one common workspace.

The Infini Capital Command Center reads that shared state and turns distributed activity into a clear operational view for research, monitoring, and decision support.

Blackboard architectureA design pattern where independent knowledge sources contribute to a shared workspace, allowing other components to consume relevant information without tight direct coupling.

03

Specialized by design

Different components can focus on different questions while contributing to the same operational picture.

Research

Investigates patterns, tests ideas, and produces analytical tasks or findings.

Market analysis

Processes price, volume, universe, and other market context relevant to a workflow.

Risk and controls

Reviews signals and decisions against defined constraints and operating rules.

Monitoring

Tracks task progress, agent activity, errors, and system health.

Operations

Coordinates approved workflows and keeps execution-related state visible.

Command Center

Brings the distributed state together for human review and oversight.

04

Why this architecture

Less coupling

Agents do not need to know the internal implementation of every other agent. They interact through shared work and state.

More visibility

Tasks, events, messages, and outcomes provide a traceable record of how work moved through the system.

Independent evolution

One analytical component can improve without requiring the whole system to be rewritten.

Operational context

The Command Center provides a practical view of distributed research and automation activity.

The principle: contribute information where it is produced, make it available where it is needed, and keep the workflow visible to the people responsible for it.
05

Built for disciplined iteration

A trading system is not one calculation or one model. It is a continuous process of gathering information, testing ideas, assessing conditions, recording decisions, and learning from results.

The Blackboard gives that process a shared operational surface—structured enough for automation, visible enough for oversight, and flexible enough for new research workflows.

Traceable: work and outcomes remain connected.
Composable: new capabilities can join the system.
Observable: activity is visible in the Command Center.
Controlled: automation remains within defined workflows.
06

A practical blackboard

For Infini Capital, blackboard architecture is not a visual metaphor alone. It is a way to organize the relationship between data, research, agents, operational state, and human oversight.
