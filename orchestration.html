01

From signal to action

Infini Capital's orchestration layer sits between market context and trading action. It takes signals produced by research and analysis workflows, applies defined checks, and coordinates the resulting operational steps in a controlled sequence.

The layer is designed to make automation reliable: every signal follows the same path, every check is recorded, and every action remains within defined operating rules.

Signal orchestrationA design approach where market signals, validation rules, and operational actions are coordinated through a shared, traceable workflow rather than handled as isolated events.
02

How a signal moves

Each signal follows a defined sequence from context to recorded outcome. No stage fires out of order, and no action proceeds without passing the required controls.

01 / CONTEXT

Market context

Relevant market data and conditions are gathered and normalized.

02 / SIGNAL

Signal

A candidate signal is produced from the available context.

03 / CHECK

Controls

Defined risk and sanity checks are applied before proceeding.

04 / ACTION

Action

Approved workflows carry out the resulting operational step.

05 / RECORD

Record

Outcome and state are logged for review and learning.

Every stage writes to a shared operational trail, enabling full post-trade reconstruction.

03

Components of the layer

The orchestration layer is built from specialized components, each owning a distinct part of the workflow while contributing to the same operational picture.

Context engine

Gathers and normalizes market data and conditions relevant to the current workflow.

Signal interface

Receives candidate signals and prepares them for validation.

Control gate

Applies risk, sanity, and operating-rule checks before any action proceeds.

Execution coordinator

Carries out approved actions and tracks their status.

State store

Persists workflow state, outcomes, and audit history locally.

Review trail

Records every decision point for post-trade analysis and debugging.

04

Why this design

Consistency

Every signal follows the same validated path, reducing manual error and unpredictable behavior.

Traceability

Full audit history connects context, signal, check, action, and outcome.

Controlled automation

Actions only proceed within defined rules, with fail-safe defaults when in doubt.

Scalable structure

The same layer supports multiple workflows without rewriting core logic.

The principle: no signal becomes an action without passing defined controls, and no action occurs without leaving a record.
05

Built for operational discipline

Trading automation is not about removing humans from the loop—it is about making the loop reliable enough that human oversight focuses on the right questions.

The orchestration layer gives that oversight a structured surface: clear stages, recorded decisions, and defined boundaries for what automation can and cannot do.

Repeatable: the same input produces the same controlled path.
Auditable: every decision point is recorded.
Bounded: automation operates within defined limits.
Reviewable: outcomes feed directly into post-trade learning.
06

A practical layer

For Infini Capital, signal orchestration is not a metaphor. It is the operational bridge between research output and disciplined trading action—structured enough for automation, visible enough for oversight, and flexible enough to evolve with new workflows.
